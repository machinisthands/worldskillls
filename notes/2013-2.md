This test project from WorldSkills 2013 in Leipzig is a two-sided part: the drawing shows features on both the top and the bottom, so it needs at least two setups. Use **Show bottom** on the 3D model to see the second side.

## Key features (from the drawing)
- Overall size 97 × 147 mm
- **M30 × 1.5** internal thread
- Two precision bores: **Ø24 H7** (24.000–24.021) and **Ø22 H7** (22.000–22.021)
- General tolerance ±0.1 mm unless stated otherwise
- All edges broken 0.2–0.3 × 45°
- Surface finish Ra 0.8, with Ra 1.6 where indicated

## Things to watch
- H7 bores need a finishing operation (reaming or fine boring). Check them with a bore gauge or plug gauge, not only a caliper.
- Plan the setups so that the bottom features can be located from surfaces finished in the first setup.
- Break every edge to 0.2–0.3 × 45° before the part leaves the machine. It is easy to miss small edges inside pockets.
