Test Project 2 from the WorldSkills 2011 competition in London. Use Show bottom on the 3D model to see the underside.

Key features (from the drawing)

  • Overall size about 145 × 95 × 46 mm
  • Threads: M30 × 1.5, M10
  • Fits: Ø10 H7, Ø12 H7, Ø20 H7
  • Surface finish Ra 1.6, with Ra 0.8 where marked
  • Material: steel C45E (stock 150 × 100 × 50 mm)
